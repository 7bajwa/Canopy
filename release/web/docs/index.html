How Canopy works

A plant is a graph of nodes. A Spline node is one part of the plant: a line (its length and steps, bent by modifiers), where those lines start (Placement), what wraps them (a tube, leaf blades or cutout leaves), how they look (a material) and how they move (wind). A Mesh node is a solid shape placed as copies, which other parts can also grow on.

Connect a node's Children output to another node's Parent input to grow the second from the first. Each setting can vary from copy to copy (±) and follow a curve along its line or up the chain (〜). A setting's sockets start hidden: right-click a value to expose it, copy, paste or reset it.

The editor

  • Toolbar (top) — File (new, open a file or a sample, save, backup, import, export), undo and redo, add a Spline, Mesh or Mix node, materials and the cutout editor, layout, shading, wireframe, and Render · Bake · Pack. Each group can be folded into one button or hidden in Settings › Toolbar. Drag its grip to dock it on any edge.
  • Node graph (left) — the plant as nodes. A node's eye switches it on and off; its tabs hold its settings. Right-click empty space to add a node.
  • 3D view (right) — the plant itself. Click a part to pick its node.
  • Navigation icons — pan, zoom and frame each view without a mouse; drag their grip to dock them on a wall.
  • Gizmo and dials (top corner of the 3D view) — click an axis to look along it; the dials turn the light and the wind (click the middle to switch one off); the ruler shows the scale figure.

    Exporting

    The Export window picks the format (GLB, FBX, or OBJ in a zip), whether pictures are packed inside the file, and whether the plant goes out as one mesh, one mesh per node, or each piece on its own with its pivot at its base.

    • Vertex colors — each of R, G, B and A can carry a gradient: height, along each branch, along each leaf, or a random number per piece.
    • Pivot Painter 2 — two EXR textures (pivot position + parent index, X axis + extent); each piece points at its pixel in the second UV set.
    • Vertex animation — the wind baked as offsets per frame into an EXR, read through the third UV set.
    • Bone rig (GLB) — one bone per branch; leaves ride the branch they grow on.

    Bake and Pack write texture maps. Each map has a checkbox in front and its settings under the arrow at its end: the suffix added to the file name, Dilate (bleeds colors past the edges so mipmaps don't show a fringe), and its own extras. The Packed texture puts any maps into one file's R, G, B and A.
