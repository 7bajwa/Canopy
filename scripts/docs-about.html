About Canopy

Canopy is a node-based editor for trees, plants and foliage. Build a plant from nodes, shape it with modifiers, watch it sway in the wind, and export it ready for games and films. It runs in the browser, on Windows and on Android tablets.

Created by Amanpreet Bajwa, with passion and AI. From an artist, for artists.
