Working in Canopy

Saving and backups

  • Save writes over the file you have open. Save As picks a new name or place.
  • Canopy makes a project folder in your Documents (Documents/Canopy). It holds your backups and temporary files.
  • Automatic backup runs every 15 minutes; change it in Settings › System. A plant that was never saved backs up as Canopy-YYMMDD_HHMM.canopy; a saved one as <file name>_YYMMDD_HHMM.canopy.
  • In the browser, a backup is a download, so Canopy asks first.

Welcome window

The Welcome window opens when Canopy starts. You can turn it off there.

Windows and panels

Move any window by its title bar and resize it from its corner. Canopy remembers each size and place until you press Reset the layout in Settings › Interface.

Status bar and tooltips

The bar at the bottom shows what is under the pointer (a node, a tab, a value) and what each tool does. Set its size or turn it off in Settings › Interface. Hover tooltips can be turned off, and their delay changed (3 s to start).

Variation

Each value's ± box has two modes:

  • Percent — varies by a share of the value. It does nothing to a value of 0.
  • Absolute — adds or takes away up to that amount. It works even at 0.

Settings can give every new node a starting variation, for example 10%.

Toolbar

Drag the toolbar by its grip. When it doesn't fit, scroll it; drag its end to make more rows. Click the Canopy leaf to fold it away, and click it again to bring it back.

Shortcuts

Settings › Shortcuts lists every shortcut and lets you change them. Some commands start without a key, so you can add your own. Settings › Navigation can open tabs and toolbar lists when you hover over them.

Units

Pick metres, centimetres, millimetres, or feet and inches (as decimals or fractions) in Settings › System.

Scale figure

A figure stands beside the plant to show its size: from a squirrel (25 cm) to a giraffe (5.5 m) and a Sauroposeidon (18 m). Pick which ones show in Settings › 3D view.
